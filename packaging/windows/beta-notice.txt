TONE LEGION MULTIBAND - BETA BUILD

Thanks for testing. Please read this before you install.

* This is a test build, not a finished product. It may crash your DAW,
  sound wrong or change between builds. Save your sessions often and
  don't use it on a project you can't afford to lose.

* Saved settings may not carry over. Parameters and saved state can still
  change between beta builds, so a session saved with one beta may not
  reload the same way in the next one.

* Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, only. This installer puts the VST3 and/or CLAP
  plugin in the standard Common Files folders. A Mac build is separate.

* The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn
  that it comes from an unknown publisher. See "Tester notes.txt" for
  what to click.

  anywhere; ask the person who sent it to you if someone else would
  like to test.
